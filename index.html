Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know About Unpublished Fares

What are unpublished airfares and how does Antelmo access them?

Unpublished airfares (also called wholesale or consolidator fares) are private contract rates negotiated directly with major international airlines by consolidators like ASAP Tickets. Airlines restrict these fares from public price-comparison engines to preserve their retail pricing, and authorize licensed agents like Antelmo to distribute them via direct telephone inquiries.

Can Antelmo place courtesy holds on my tickets?

Yes. Depending on airline fare rules and departure proximity, Antelmo can frequently hold seats and lock in quoted fares for 24 to 72 hours without upfront payment, allowing you to confirm plans with family or employers.

What happens if my airline reschedules or delays my flight?

You do not have to wait in hours-long airline telephone queues. As your dedicated travel manager, Antelmo directly accesses airline booking systems to arrange rebooking, rerouting, or schedule adjustments according to airline carriage regulations.
